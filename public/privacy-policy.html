Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 29, 2026

Sri Balaji Renewables ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Sri Balaji Renewables POS mobile application (the "App") under Rajugari Ventures. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our App.

1. Information Collection and Use

Sri Balaji Renewables POS is an enterprise point-of-sale and inventory management application designed for authorized store personnel and business administrators. We collect and process the following information solely for business operations:

  • User Credentials: Username and authentication credentials to facilitate secure login.
  • Transaction & Order Data: Sales receipts, product inventory levels, and customer purchase records.
  • Camera Permission: Used exclusively within the App for barcode scanning of inventory products. Camera video streams are processed locally in real time and are not stored or transmitted to external servers.

2. Data Security

The security of your data is important to us. We implement industry-standard SSL/TLS encryption for all data transmitted between the App and our secure servers.

3. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain transaction and business records as required for store accounting and inventory compliance. You may request account deletion or data clarification by contacting our administrator.

4. Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal or transaction data with any third-party advertisers or external data brokers.
